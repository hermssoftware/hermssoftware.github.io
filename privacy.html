Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 26, 2026

Herms Software ("we," "our," or "us") operates the mobile application ECHO-TERRA: COMMAND (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information Collection and Use

While using our App, we do not require you to create an account or provide personal identification information such as your name or home address. However, our third-party service providers may collect certain information automatically to provide essential app functions.

Types of Data Collected:

  • Device Identifiers: We may collect information about your device, including your mobile device ID (e.g., Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA), model, and operating system version.
  • Usage Data: Information on how the App is accessed and used (e.g., app launches, taps, and gameplay progress).
  • Diagnostics: Performance data, such as crash logs and battery usage, to help us improve the game.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

We use third-party companies to facilitate our App and provide services on our behalf. These third parties have access to your data only to perform these tasks and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

  • Google AdMob: We use AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use your device’s advertising ID to show personalized ads.
    Google Privacy & Terms
  • Capacitor Framework: Our app uses the Capacitor framework to handle native device features (like Haptics and Status Bar). This framework does not store personal data on its own.

3. Advertising and Opt-Out

We use your device's advertising identifier to serve ads that are relevant to you. You can opt-out of interest-based advertising or reset your advertising ID through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy > Tracking (to disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track").

4. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will delete it immediately.

5. Data Security and Retention

The security of your data is important to us. All data transmitted to third-party services (like AdMob) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS). We do not store your personal data on our own servers.
